## SERVICES

### Live Caricature Art

Live caricature art for parties, events, booths, activations, and celebrations.

Rates start at $175/hr. Scott draws 12-15 faces per hour and can customize paper with party info, event date, theme, school name, company logo, or brand message.

Scott brings:

- Chair and lighting
- Paper, markers, and bags
- Sturdy card stock
- Clear plastic sleeves
- Optional frames and hard vinyl sleeves

What Scott needs:

- 8x8 area, large enough for chair, easel, and guests
- Seating for guests
- Parking
- Easy access without steep inclines
- No heavy furniture to move
- No blasting music or strobe lights

Scott does not need a table or bottles of water.

Scott does not carry supplemental insurance and will not purchase it.

### Coloring Activity Parties

All-ages art activity parties using black-and-white coloring pages. Scott brings markers, art stix, lap boards, paper, bags, and custom coloring pages that match the party theme.

Guests color their own artwork. Scott visits each artist, shows appreciation, demonstrates techniques, adds flair, and helps everyone leave with a drawing they love.

Pricing note: 1-hour ($200) deposit required for coloring parties, discount for 2+ hours.

### Theme Parties

Theme parties with template paper and characters matching the event theme. Scott leaves enough room to draw guest faces into the design. Strong fit for astronaut, dinosaur, sports, Disney princess, monster truck, Marvel superhero, and similar themes.

### Color Commissions

Full-color portraits from photos with custom themes. Good for family portraits, astronaut themes, dinosaur themes, sports themes, Disney princess themes, monster truck themes, Marvel superhero themes, t-shirt designs, family reunion art, and family cruise art.

### Figure Drawing Parties

Figure drawing parties are available when appropriate for the audience and event format.
